Mob Types

Every mob–type mapping from the wiki's Mob Types/List page, merged with every mob–location mapping from its Bestiary/List page (parsed from raw wikitext). Types in gold have a matching enchantment that grants bonus damage against them — everything else is flavor/lore classification only. Locations in green are mostly Bestiary tabs; the six Fishing-related ones are split into Sea Creatures - Water, Sea Creatures - Lava, Sea Creatures - Spooky, Sea Creatures - Fishing Festival, Sea Creatures - Winter, and Sea Creatures - Backwater Bayou (the Bestiary's own Spooky Festival fishing catches, not its land-based Spooky Festival tab). A Slayer location is also included for the 33 Slayer bosses / Dungeon-only mobs the Bestiary itself doesn't track, hand-mapped rather than parsed.
